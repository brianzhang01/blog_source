I had a wonderful time with [Brown Puzzle Hunt (BPH) 2026](https://www.brownpuzzlehunt.com/). My wife and I solved with a great group of remote friends and regulars at the local Puzzled Pint, many of whom were doing a longer-than-Puzzled-Pint hunt for the first time. I thought the puzzle quality was quite high with elegant ideas and clean execution, even if the overall structure didn't have the same magic I felt from BPH 2025.

Here are my favorites! (**Bold** = an all-time favorite.)

## Metas, ranked
1. **[Anthropology of Computers](https://www.brownpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/anthropology-of-computers)**
    - Sam wrote a puzzle involving the 2 core mechanics for the Bay Area High School Puzzle Hunt back in 2010-2011 days, with the flavortext "good luck in the next iteration of life". I'm surprised to not have seen those ideas executed as a meta until now! I ended up [writing code](https://gist.github.com/brianzhang01/3ec09265face7ba5997572146c0da30d) to solve it, which was helpful for cycling through hypotheses of how the puzzle worked.
2. [Theological Chemistry](https://www.brownpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/theological-chemistry)
3. [Bar Exam](https://www.brownpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/bar-exam)
    - wife had a good time mostly solo-ing it!
4. [Underwater Basketweaving](https://www.brownpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/underwater-basketweaving)
5. [Cubist Linguistics](https://www.brownpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/cubist-linguistics)

## Favorite regular puzzles I worked on, ranked
1. **[Itty Bitty Kitties: A Bi-Nya-ry Study](https://www.brownpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/itty-bitty-kitties)**
    - what a clever, simple puzzle!
2. **[Multi-Clef Composition](https://www.brownpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/multi-clef-composition)**
    - I loved the sheet music puzzles in both BPH 2025 and 2026.[^sheet] This one and [Identify the Piece](https://2025.brownpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/identify-the-piece) (BPH 2025) can be my go-to music puzzle recommendations for now.
3. **[Herculean Math](https://www.brownpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/herculean-math)**
4. **[Mischievous Literature](https://www.brownpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/mischievous-literature)**
5. **[Going in Circles](https://www.brownpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/going-in-circles)**
    - ah, when watching 3Blue1Brown finally pays off. Got to do this one with another former math Olympian.
6. [Topics in Guard-Door Theory](https://www.brownpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/topics-in-guard-door-theory)
7. [Collaborative Chorale](https://www.brownpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/collaborative-chorale)
8. [Editing Practice](https://www.brownpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/editing-practice)
